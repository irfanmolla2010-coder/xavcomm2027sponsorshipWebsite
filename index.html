The University

St. Xavier's University, Kolkata

St. Xavier’s University, Kolkata (SXUK) was born out of a vision to create a centre of excellence in higher education in West Bengal. In 2011, Rev. Fr. Dr. John Felix Raj, S.J., and the St. Xavier's College (Calcutta) Alumni Association conceived 'SXC Vision 2020', paving the way for SXUK.

The Government of West Bengal generously allocated a sprawling 17-acre campus at Rajarhat. Following the St. Xavier's University, Kolkata Act 2016, the university was formally inaugurated on July 7, 2017.

Today, SXUK stands as a testament to academic excellence, innovation, and social responsibility across Commerce, Arts, Social Studies, Xavier Business School (AICTE MBA), and Xavier Law School (BCI-approved law programs).

Festival Edition 2027

The Theme: Tapestry of Stories

Xavcomm’27 is envisioned as a multifaceted celebration of creativity, communication, and culture. With a distinctive artistic outlook, it serves as an electric platform for young communicators, storytellers, and creators to explore the ever-evolving world of media and the arts.

Rooted in the spirit of innovation and collaboration, Xavcomm provides a vibrant space for students to showcase their talents across diverse disciplines — from journalism and advertising to photography, filmmaking, and digital expression.

“Through its thoughtfully curated events and exhibitions, Xavcomm reflects the vision of Mass Communication as both an art and a responsibility — one that informs, inspires, and influences society.”

Each year, Xavcomm brings together participants from colleges across the region, uniting creative minds under one roof. It offers an exceptional opportunity for brands and organizations to connect with an energetic, media-savvy youth audience, while supporting the growth of future communicators and cultural leaders.

Journalism & Reporting

Investigative reporting, ethical journalism, news broadcast drills, and critical media critique.

Advertising & PR Strategy

Ad pitch challenges, brand crisis management, viral campaign ideation, and consumer psychology.

Cinema & Video Production

Short film competitions, scriptwriting, mobile cinematography, and documentary filmmaking.

Photography & Digital Art

Street photography, photojournalism galleries, graphic design, and short-form digital storytelling.

Official Invitation

Letter to the Sponsors

We are delighted to announce that St. Xavier’s University, Kolkata, is preparing to host the 5th edition of “Xavcomm”, the annual inter-college fest organized by the Department of Mass Communication under the Faculty of Arts and Social Studies.

Xavcomm has evolved over the years into a thriving platform that honours innovation, creativity, and teamwork in the field of media and communication. With participants from over 30 colleges and universities in the region, the 2027 edition is expected to be even more exciting. The two-day fest will feature over a dozen events spanning diverse domains including journalism, public relations, advertising, creative arts, and digital content creation.

Our fest aims to showcase the wide range of skills and imaginative potential of aspiring media professionals by providing a platform where expression can meet excellence.

With an expected footfall of 2000–3000 per day, Xavcomm 2027 offers a unique opportunity for your esteemed organisation to engage with talented and media-savvy youth — our future influencers and professionals.

We would be delighted to have your prestigious organization as a sponsor in order to make this celebration of creativity and communication a successful endeavour. We sincerely look forward to your positive response and partnership to make Xavcomm 2027 an unforgettable experience for all.
